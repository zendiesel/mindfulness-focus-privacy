Privacy Policy for Volna Radio

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Volna Radio is an Android internet radio app provided by an independent developer using the name Neuroquiet. For privacy questions and requests, contact pixelgan@gmail.com.

Information on your device

Volna stores saved stations, recently selected stations, settings and station images you choose on your device. The app may cache images. Clearing app data or uninstalling Volna removes its local app data, subject to your device’s settings. Volna does not provide accounts or cloud synchronization in this version.

Connections to radio services

To find stations, Volna sends your search terms and selected filters to Radio Browser, an independent station directory. When you start a station from its catalogue, Volna may send its station identifier to Radio Browser for its station click counter. Radio Browser says it keeps the IP addresses used by this counter for 24 hours to limit repeat counts. See Radio Browser’s FAQ.

To play a station, your device connects directly to the broadcaster or its streaming provider. Station images may be loaded from the station, directory or other image servers. These providers and their infrastructure can receive your IP address, requested resource and technical connection information. They control their own retention and processing practices; Volna does not control the content or advertising in radio streams.

Reporting a station problem

If you choose to report a problem, Volna opens the Android sharing menu with the station name and country. Information is sent when you select a destination and complete the share. The recipient’s own privacy practices apply.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

This version does not include a Volna advertising SDK, analytics SDK, user accounts or in-app purchases. Radio broadcasts may include advertising inserted by the station or its provider. Radio Browser’s station click counter records station popularity as described above.

Security and retention

Volna uses HTTPS for its supported catalogue, stream and image connections. Device data remains on your device until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or Android clears cached files. Independent providers determine how long they retain information they receive; the 24-hour period stated above applies to Radio Browser’s click counter, not necessarily to other logs or providers.

Privacy requests

Contact pixelgan@gmail.com for questions or requests about your data. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict processing, or complain to a data protection authority. For information independently held by a broadcaster or other provider, contact that provider directly. We may update this policy if the app or its data practices change.
